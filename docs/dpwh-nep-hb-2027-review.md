# Review: csiiiv/DPWH-NEP-HB-2027-ANALYSIS

What the external repository contains, how it reconciles against our FY2027
NEP dataset, and what is worth bringing into the `/2027` microsite.

- Repository: https://github.com/csiiiv/DPWH-NEP-HB-2027-ANALYSIS
- Hosted app: https://csiiiv.github.io/DPWH-NEP-HB-2027-ANALYSIS/app/
- First reviewed 11 October 2026 at commit `cf61c93`. Re-reviewed
  **23 October 2026 at commit `a0caa77`** (77 further commits, 138 total).
- Scope has grown from DPWH only to four programs: **DPWH** (NEP vs House Bill
  10858 vs the DPWH Transparency listing), **DA farm-to-market roads**,
  **DOH Health Facilities Enhancement Program (HFEP)**, and **NIA irrigation**,
  plus a cross-dataset GPS map and a DEO-to-legislative-district crosswalk.

## What it is

A set of Python extraction pipelines plus one React/Vite workbench on GitHub
Pages. The Python side turns budget PDFs into additive trees with per-row PDF
page provenance, then matches NEP rows to House rows. Every dataset carries
input SHA-256 hashes and a `comparison_ready: false` flag. The authors are
explicit that unmatched rows are candidates, not policy claims, and on
23 October added a full public disclaimer under `disclaimer/README.md` saying
the same in plain language.

The repository is 1.4 GB because it commits the House PDFs, OCR dumps and a
722-page NEP OCR PDF. The analytical JSON under `analysis/data/` is 415 MB.
The files we would actually consume total about 100 MB for DPWH and under
3 MB for the three annexes.

The browser payloads (`main_overview_2027.json`, `comparison_overview_2027.json`,
`comparison_projects_2027.json`, the `*_workbench.json` files, the FMR final
datasets and the congress index) are **not committed**. They are regenerated by
Node builders at `npm run dev` or `npm run build`. The retained ledgers below
are committed and are the citable inputs.

## Reconciliation against our FY2027 NEP data

Our `/2027` data comes from the DBM `NEP-FY2027.csv`. Theirs comes from OCR
or native text of the printed volumes. Everything below agrees exactly once
scope is aligned.

### DPWH department total and expense classes

| | Ours (CSV, dept 18) | Theirs (PDF tree) | Difference | Explanation |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| DPWH total | ₱643,953,276,000 | ₱642,612,015,000 | ₱1,341,261,000 | Our total includes automatic appropriations; theirs is new appropriations only (NEP p.7 vs p.8) |
| Personnel services | ₱16,235,385,000 | ₱14,922,297,000 | ₱1,313,088,000 | Retirement and Life Insurance Premiums (fund `10401102`) |
| MOOE | ₱24,713,919,000 | ₱24,685,746,000 | ₱28,173,000 | National Building Code Development Fund (fund `10403389`) |
| Capital outlays | ₱603,003,972,000 | ₱603,003,972,000 | 0 | |

Our two automatic-appropriation fund rows sum to exactly the difference.
Their source audit records the same split.

### DPWH programs and PAPs

Mapping our PREXC prefixes to their eight program totals: the six operations
programs (Asset Preservation, Network Development, Bridge, Flood Management,
Convergence, Local) match to the peso. GAS and STO differ by the same
₱1,341,261,000 of automatic appropriations.

Their 45 non-FAP PAP controls against our program rows: 28 match to the
peso, 10 differ by exactly the foreign-assisted component our CSV folds into
the same PREXC code, and 7 have different labels but sum correctly. No
unexplained difference. The DPWH NEP tree and the House I-C extracts have
**identical hashes** on 11 and 23 October, so this reconciliation still holds.

### The three annexes added since 11 October

| DOH HFEP, capital outlays | ₱14,472,659,000 (464 items) | DOH activity `350202100002000`, capital outlays | Exact. Our remaining ₱67,173,000 is MOOE, which they document as outside the annex |
| NIA irrigation, six sub-programs | ₱17,598,181,000 (791 projects) | BSGC agency `35-005`, six-digit PREXC prefixes | All six match to the peso: NIS ₱4,662,231,000, CIS ₱1,620,000,000, Other ₱3,000,000,000, Pump ₱7,915,950,000, Special ₱200,000,000, Extension ₱200,000,000 |

Our CSV is an independent check on all four of their NEP extractions, and
their trees are project-level decompositions of our numbers.

### Operating units

Our 217 DPWH operating units vs their 222 office labels: 194 match after
whitespace and case normalisation. The remainder are ordinal spellings,
"Regional Office X - Proper" vs "Regional Office X", and OCR artefacts. A
small alias table joins them.

## What it adds that we do not have

1. **Project grain.** The DBM CSV stops at the activity level. Their DPWH NEP
   tree has 11,317 project nodes with titles, chainages, region, implementing
   office and PDF page. FMR, HFEP and NIA get the same treatment at 704, 464
   and 791 rows.
2. **Geography.** The CSV codes Central Office projects to NCR, so our DPWH
   regions tab shows ₱520.8B in NCR. The printed NEP groups by region first:
   NCR ₱159.1B, Region IV-A ₱52.0B, Region III ₱42.7B, Region X ₱42.5B.
   FMR rows carry printed coordinates, normalised and geocoded to PSGC.
3. **The House stage.** HB 10858 at 2nd and 3rd reading for DPWH
   (₱654,102,015,000, about 16,000 named projects) and the three annexes
   (FMR ₱19,628,100,000, HFEP ₱10,021,659,000, NIA ₱17,999,305,000). The
   annex pages were verified text-identical between the two readings.
4. **NEP to House matching** with a disciplined vocabulary. Since 22 October
   it also refuses to book same-road pairs with non-overlapping chainage as
   amount changes, and cross-marks identical-title twins under different PAPs
   instead of auto-pairing them.
5. **2nd to 3rd reading diff.** Five new Caloocan projects (₱134M) funded by
   an equal cut to Right-of-Way. Unchanged since 11 October.
6. **BetterGov Transparency API reconciliation.** 23 printed NEP allocations
   totalling ₱9.797B are absent from `api.dpwh.bettergov.ph`, and all FAP is
   out of its scope. Direct feedback for our own DPWH transparency project.
7. **DEO to legislative district crosswalk** (`DEO2LEG/`). 200 offices, 254
   districts, 264 relationships, representatives verified against the
   congress.gov.ph directory on 22 October 2026 with 252 resolved and 2
   ambiguous. Comes with strict usage rules: label every figure Tier A (office
   serves one district) or Tier B (shared, never divided), and always publish
   the unattributable Central and Regional Office residual, which is 78% of
   the NEP side and 70% of the House side. The Congress tab is hidden in their
   UI as of 23 October but the data and routes remain.
8. **Chainage parsing** into peso-per-kilometre peer benchmarks, and a GPS
   inventory of 61,688 records yielding 9,827 parsed points.
9. **Hearing-oriented writing.** `analysis-nia/SENATE_HEARING_BRIEF.md` is a
   one-page brief built from the ledgers. A model for what our hearings pages
   could link to.

## What to reuse

**Datasets, pinned to a commit.**

| File | Size | Rows | Use |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `analysis/data/nep_2027_source_projects.json` | 6 MB | 11,420 | DPWH NEP project list with page, region, office, PAP, FAP flag |
| `analysis/data/house_reading_changes_2027.json` | 39 MB | 16,272 | DPWH House 2nd vs 3rd reading |
| `analysis/data/stage_trace_2027.json` | 56 MB | 17,611 | DPWH unified NEP / House / Transparency rows with match status |
| `other-analysis/data/shareable/{hgab,nep}_fmr_projects_normalized.csv` | 0.7 MB | 964 + 704 | FMR projects with normalised coordinates |
| `analysis-nia/data/hgab_nia_projects.csv`, `nep_nia_dataset.json` | 1.2 MB | 828 + 791 | NIA irrigation projects by sub-program |
| `analysis-hfep/data/{hgab,nep}_hfep_facilities.csv` | 0.4 MB | 513 + 464 | HFEP facilities with four funding columns |
| `DEO2LEG/outputs/deo_leg_crosswalk.json` | 0.6 MB | 264 | Office to district to representative graph, no amounts |

**Fit with our architecture.** DPWH is about 45,000 rows; the annexes add
about 4,300. Load the small tables into D1 beside the `nep_*` tables under a
new prefix, and put the 17,611-row DPWH project table in parquet on R2 for
DuckDB-Wasm search. Record their input SHA-256 hashes in our manifest and
re-run the reconciliation above as verifier invariants on every refresh.

**Pages.** `/2027/d/18` gains a House-bill tab (PAP controls NEP vs HB 2nd vs
HB 3rd), a Projects tab (searchable, with match status and PDF page), and a
region view that uses printed geography. `/2027/d/05`, `/2027/d/13` and
`/2027/d/35` gain a single annex tab each: FMR, HFEP, NIA, with the House
stage beside the NEP stage. Keep their status vocabulary and the "candidate,
not insertion" framing verbatim, and link their disclaimer.

**Ideas worth copying, not code.** Shareable filter URLs, the per-row PDF
page reference, the hash-manifest gate that refuses stale inputs, the rule
that unmatched rows are never labelled insertions or cuts, and the Tier A /
Tier B attribution rule for anything district-level.

## What not to reuse

- Their SPA. Hash-routed JSX for GitHub Pages with its own PDF.js viewer,
  MapLibre map and Node payload builders. Our React Router / Worker / D1 stack
  is different enough that porting costs more than rebuilding.
- The Python NEP builders for DPWH. They require an external
  `paddle_pdf_ocr_v2` directory that is not in the repository. The annex
  extractors are self-contained but we only need their outputs.
- Anything under `analysis/archive/`, the v5 OCR-era House extract, and the
  ADRs now filed under `analysis/docs/adr - DEFUNCT/`.
- The 324 scraped representative portraits under `DEO2LEG/legislative_profiles/`
  and `analysis/data/congress_portraits/`. Reuse rights are unclear.

## Risks and blockers

1. **No licence.** Still no LICENSE file at `a0caa77`; GitHub reports
   `license: null`. The new disclaimer covers accuracy, not reuse. The FMR
   "shareable" package has no terms either. Ask csiiiv for a licence (MIT for
   code, CC-BY for data) before shipping any of it. Their provenance paths
   reference a `BetterGovPH/Budget-NEP` workspace, their DEO2LEG work builds on
   `bettergovph/open-data-visualization`, and they fetch from our API, so the
   author is already in the BetterGov orbit.
2. **Provisional matching.** `comparison_ready: false` everywhere. The match
   vocabulary gained four statuses in twelve days and the counts moved. Carry
   the candidate framing and pin a commit.
3. **Moving target.** 77 commits in twelve days. Builders are rerun often and
   the browser payloads are not committed, so a "number on their site" is not
   reproducible without a commit hash.
4. **Third-party inputs in DEO2LEG.** The allocation table is a PCIJ-sourced
   scan with no fiscal year, and the roster and portraits are scraped from
   congress.gov.ph. Fine as a lookup; do not quote its amounts as FY2027.
5. **Size.** Do not vendor the repository. Fetch the files above by raw URL at
   a pinned commit and keep them out of Git.

## Suggested next steps

1. Contact the author about licensing and whether they want this hosted at
   bettergov.ph. Mention the four exact reconciliations; it is a useful
   result for them too.
2. Pin `a0caa77`, download the files in the table above, and write an importer
   that emits `data/2027/stages/` plus a D1 script, asserting the DPWH, FMR,
   HFEP and NIA reconciliations as verifier invariants.
3. Prototype the DPWH House-bill tab with PAP controls only, then the three
   annex tabs, which are small and flat.
4. Pass the 23 missing allocations and the FAP gap to the DPWH transparency
   API team.
5. Revisit the DEO crosswalk once the hearings pages need a district angle,
   and only under their four conditions.
