# Philippine General Appropriations Act (GAA), FY2000–FY2026

This table holds every amount we could read from 27 years of the General Appropriations Act, the budget law Congress passes each year. That is {rows} rows in one schema, with amounts in exact pesos. Each row points back to the DBM document it came from.

> **Independent and unofficial.** This is BetterGov's reading of the published GAA documents, not an official record. Amounts are appropriations, meaning what the law authorizes. They are not obligations, releases or spending. Detail changes from year to year, so read the coverage table before you compare years.

## Before you compare years

- **The level of detail changes:**
  - 2015–2018 and 2020–2026 are line items by program, office, fund, expense class and object.
  - 2019 is by agency and object.
  - 2009–2013 is by program, area and expense class.
  - 2000–2008 and 2014 are agency, program or fund totals.
  - The `grain` column says which kind each row is.
- **Three years had no new GAA.** In 2001, 2004 and 2006 the previous year's GAA was reenacted (the 2000, 2003 and 2005 GAAs). DBM confirmed this in an [FOI response](https://www.foi.gov.ph/requests/gaa-for-1987-2006/). Those years have no rows and a null total in the coverage table. That means no separate new GAA was published, not a zero budget. We copy no amounts forward.
- **Coverage before 2009 is partial.** The national total for those years is not established, and some automatic appropriations are missing.
- **Keep scopes apart:**
  - `unprogrammed_appropriations` can only be spent under conditions, so keep it apart from programmed amounts.
  - In some early years, the new-appropriation tables include the IRA (the local government share) or debt interest. Later years leave those out.
  - In FY2003, the first-preference and second-preference appropriations are separate scopes, not duplicates of each other.
- **FY2019 editions differ for DPWH.** The technical edition prints ₱454,016,273,000 and the Volume I summary prints ₱549,390,514,000. We use the technical edition. We do not add a balancing amount, and we do not say what the difference is.
- **Codes are not matched across decades.** Older department, agency and object codes are kept as printed. Where an older object code is not a modern UACS code, it goes in `source_object_code`.
- **What is excluded:** debt principal amortization annexes and the operating budgets of government corporations.

## How this relates to bettergovph/gaa

[bettergovph/gaa](https://huggingface.co/datasets/bettergovph/gaa) holds the FY2020–2026 extract in its original 22-column form, with 4,538,396 rows. It includes the program, agency and fund heading rows.

This dataset keeps only the 3,722,170 rows that carry an object code. The program heading for each line goes in `program_code` and `program_dsc`. The heading rows add nothing to the totals, so each year's total matches to the peso.

The `gaa` copy also has 11 rows more than the extract this dataset was built from (4 in 2022, 2 in 2024, 2 in 2025 and 3 in 2026), with the same totals.

## Sources and method

- FY2000–2019: the [DBM GAA archive](https://www.dbm.gov.ph/index.php/budget-documents-archives#gaa). Collection followed the GAA panel and the article bodies it links to, including technical editions, annexes and the accompanying BESF tables. We downloaded alternate editions and schedules, but they do not count as amounts automatically.
- Formats: legacy CSVs, technical spreadsheets, and agency and object tables printed in PDFs. Scanned summaries were read with OCR, plus a vision model for some control pages. Every model response was checked against the printed totals, and no truncated response became a fact.
- FY2020–2026: BetterGov's GAA extract, normalized into this schema with counts and totals unchanged.
- Release checks:
  - one schema for every year, and a unique `record_id` on every row;
  - no null amounts, grains or scopes;
  - no source selected twice;
  - 2020–2026 unchanged;
  - the consolidated file and the yearly files have the same row counts and totals.
- Pipeline: `scripts/gaa-history/` in [bettergovph/ai-budget]({pipeline_repo}).
